- `/glp-1-medications`
- all 12 medication pages under `/glp-1-medications/[slug]`

## Executive summary

The GLP-1 section is now structurally strong for crawlability and topical targeting, but it still needs another refinement pass to feel fully premium and medically trustworthy for users.

### Overall status

- **GEO / local signals:** Good baseline

## Main issues found

## Priority 1 — E-E-A-T is not strong enough yet for YMYL-style pages
These pages are in a health / medication-adjacent category. For that reason, stronger trust signals are important.

### Missing or weak items
- No visible **author / reviewer block** on the GLP-1 hub or medication subpages
- No visible **last reviewed / updated date**
- No visible **clinical review statement** such as reviewed by Dietitian Tejal
- No visible **source references** for medication-related guidance or safety framing
- No visible **scope disclaimer near the top** beyond the quick answer

### Why this matters
For health-adjacent pages, Google and AI systems both respond better when pages clearly show:
- who wrote / reviewed the content
- what credentials they have
- when the page was last reviewed
- whether claims are grounded in a credible framework

### Recommendation
Add a visible block on the hub and all medication pages:
- Reviewed by Dietitian Tejal
- credentials
- location / service scope
- last reviewed date
- short statement: nutrition support does not replace prescribing care

## Priority 2 — Some visible headings still sound slightly SEO/editorial
A lot of the worst language has been removed, but some headings still feel system-like rather than client-facing.

Examples that are acceptable but not ideal:
- “Quick search answers for X users”
- “Core nutrition themes”
- “How support usually helps”
- “Direct answers”

These are not wrong, but they do not feel as natural as stronger client-facing alternatives.

### Recommendation
Use more service-led headings such as:
- What to focus on while taking Wegovy
- Common food concerns on Ozempic
- Meal patterns that usually help on Rybelsus
- When to speak with your prescriber

## Priority 3 — Template repetition across all medication pages is high
All 12 subpages use the same content structure.

That is good for consistency, but it creates two risks:
- pages can feel templated to users
- pages can look lightly differentiated to search systems if not enough unique depth is added

### Current strengths
- the hero image is unique
- the medication-specific text is different
- FAQs vary by medication

### Remaining concern
The overall pattern is still very similar:
- hero
- support section
- nutrition priorities
- support cards
- direct answers
- safety block
- FAQs
- CTA
- related pages

### Recommendation
Over time, add one unique section per medication where relevant, for example:
- **Rybelsus:** morning routine and breakfast timing
- **Ozempic / Victoza / Trulicity:** blood-sugar-aware eating
- **Wegovy / Zepbound / Saxenda:** weight-management routine and sustaining intake
- **Byetta:** meal-linked timing strategies
- **Foundayo:** flexible oral routine vs fasting tablet routines

## Priority 4 — No citation layer for medication-related claims
The content is cautious, which is good, but the pages do not currently show citations or visible references.

### Recommendation
For at least the hub and major pages, add a small reviewed-content note or sources section referencing:
- FDA labeling / drug indication pages
- NIDDK / NIH weight-management information
- clear statement that prescribing decisions stay with the treating clinician

Even if you do not want heavy references on-page, a small “medical review and references” block helps E-E-A-T and AI trust.

## Priority 5 — GEO is good, but could be more service-specific
Current location signals are clear, but they are generic.

### Recommendation
Work in natural service-location language such as:
- Dietitian support for people taking Ozempic in Mumbai, Thane and online across India
- Online GLP-1 nutrition support for Indian clients who want practical home-food guidance

This should stay natural and not become repetitive location stuffing.
